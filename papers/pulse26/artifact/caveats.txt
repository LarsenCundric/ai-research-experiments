     Local tide RULED OUT: the Praia envelope gives -0.32, and the Praia/Limbe envelope correlation is 0.955.
     Local swell RULED OUT: the control gives -0.27, and Hs is flat at 1.38/1.36/1.37 m across the terciles.
     Selection RULED OUT: the detection rate is flat across the terciles, and the metric is continuous.

 (e) Far-station metric: background regression changes gamma by +-0.3-0.7 at the strong stations

 BEST EXPLANATION: the 2-yr window has low power. On top of that there is a shared (source- or ambient-noise) fluctuation in 2022-24 that

 PAPER: "In 2-yr windows gamma is poorly constrained (sd 0.3-0.5). The 2022-24 values are low at all stations, partly because the stations

CAVEAT 2 - gamma < 1
 (a) Errors in variables: MINOR. With the true tide at Sao Tome or Lagos, the recovered gamma is 0.93-0.94; with S2 off by +-20%, 0.89/1.12.
     The observed Port Sonara S2/M2 (2008-11) is 0.344, vs 0.361 in the prediction.
     Using other gauges' envelopes gives EDA 0.69-0.77 and TAM 0.69-0.75. This explains at most ~0.05-0.1.
 (b) dB/log compression: RULED OUT as a cause of gamma < 1. Linear-in-power responses give gamma 1.01-1.80 (always >= 1).
 (c) Shape: EDA bins accept proportional (chi2 7.7/7, p = 0.36). Power law (0.70), linear+offset (offset 0.15 dB) and saturating fits are
     marginally better (dAIC ~ 1-2), and constant is rejected (p = 0.001). TAM bins scatter beyond their errors, so all fits fail
     (p = 0.002) and the shape is undecidable.
     Symmetric saturation tanh(h/0.6 m) reproduces gamma 0.70 with M4/M2 0.046 (observed 0.071, tide 0.054): ALLOWED.
     One-sided clipping or thresholds: RULED OUT (M4/M2 0.14-0.32).
 (d) Constant non-tidal component: a solar/thermal S2 is NOT SUPPORTED. The S2 excess over the AM prediction is -0.01 [-0.40, 0.45] (EDA)
     and +0.19 [-0.34, 0.76] (TAM); the additive S2 is <= 0.08-0.11 dB. A range-independent M2-locked part (linear+offset) is ALLOWED
     but not required.
 (e) Current vs height mix: RULED OUT as a cause. Linear current gives 1.02 and u|u| gives 1.86, so any mix gives gamma >= 1.
     A delayed response is not a consistent explanation: gamma peaks at only 0.79 for a +24-36 h lag at EDA, and the best TAM lag is -12 h.
 Decomposition: the deficit comes mostly from the perigean (N2/L2) part at EDA (N2 pair 0.31 vs S2 pair 0.86; M2+S2 envelope 0.96).
     It is also metric-dependent: EDA raw line full span 1.01 vs LMB 0.70; TAM 0.32 vs 0.68.
     The EDA background bands carry significant MU2/L2-type terms (bg 20-24 s g_MU2 +0.65 [0.07, 1.13]; bg 29-34 s g_L2 -0.79 [-1.47, -0.03]).
 BEST EXPLANATION: sub-proportionality is NOT robust. The spring-neap leg alone is consistent with gamma = 1 at EDA and TAM. Below-1 values
     come from the noisy perigean leg and the background treatment. Mild symmetric saturation is the only physical mechanism tested that
     could give gamma ~ 0.7 without violating M4.
 PAPER: "The response scales with tidal range with gamma ~ 0.7-1 (EDA 0.70 [0.41, 0.99]; spring-neap-only 0.96 [0.56, 1.37]).
     Proportional scaling is not excluded. The apparent shortfall is within metric and estimation systematics."
