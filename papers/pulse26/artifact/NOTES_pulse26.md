**Headline: the M2 modulation of the 26 s line is real and it comes from the source region, not the station site.**
- G.TAM (Tamanrasset, ~2100 km inland, no ocean nearby) shows the same M2 modulation of the fixed-band (37.5-38.6 mHz) 26 s line PSD: 0.47 dB peaking at +1.6±0.2 h after M2 high water at Limbe. EDA over the same 2022-26 span: 0.49 dB at +1.1±0.3 h. With background bands regressed out: TAM 0.40 dB at +1.7 h, EDA 0.41 dB at +1.7 h (p<=0.01, red-noise surrogates; null95 ~0.06-0.08 dB). Travel time ~10 min, so the phases agree within errors. Hourly log-SNR at EDA and TAM correlates r=0.48 (72-h high-passed), so both stations see the same source. In the semidiurnal-band periodogram the M2 line is ~100x (EDA) and ~80x (TAM) the surrogate median, well above the 95% band-max level (fig3). S2 is also present at a similar lag, with a roughly tidal M2/S2 ratio. N2 is weakly present. MSf/Mf are not significant.
- Background with no 26 s peak: at EDA, 20-24 s M2 0.13 dB (p=0.07) and 29-34 s M2 0.21 dB (p=0.01, near HW). At TAM, 20-24 s shows nothing (0.02 dB) and 29-34 s shows 0.17 dB at +1.1 h. The weak 29-50 s modulation at TAM, in phase with Gulf tides, suggests that broader Gulf of Guinea long-period energy (the 28 s line's skirts / primary microseisms) is also tide-modulated. The 26 s line modulation is 2.5-4x larger than any background band at either station.
- Detection rate: EDA 26% at low water to 36% at high water (M2 term 5.5 percentage points). TAM 14% overall, M2 term 2.5 points.
- 28 s line and the EDA secondary microseism (0.1-0.3 Hz) are also M2-modulated at a similar lag. Interpretation: shallow-water ocean-wave/seafloor coupling in the Bight is tide-modulated (water depth or currents). The 26 s modulation is consistent with a source that is driven by swell in shallow water, but on its own it does NOT tell the crack model apart from a primary-microseism-like or focusing model. Also open: whether the +1.2-1.7 h lag after HW tracks tide height, current or a specific site's tide phase (the tide phase near Bioko may differ from Limbe by tens of minutes).
- Peak frequency: no robust tidal term. EDA all detections give 14 µHz (~10 ms) with per-frequency p~0.01, but it is below the band-max 95% level, gone at SNR>=2x threshold, unstable across sub-periods, and absent in the EDA 2022-26 overlap (6 µHz, p=0.5). TAM gives 38 µHz at +8.1 h (CORRECTION 10 Oct: similar phase to EDA +7.3 h, i.e. antiphase to SNR max; consistent with SNR-dependent bias, not a 'different phase'). -> treat as NULL; likely an SNR-dependent estimator bias. Upper bound ~10-15 µHz (~10 ms) M2 frequency modulation for 3-h windows.
- Long-term: the 26 s peak is a doublet, ~37.7 mHz (26.5 s) and ~38.05 mHz (26.3 s), seen in yearly median spectra at both EDA and TAM (fig7). The apparent 2025 "period jump" (yearly median 26.29-26.32 s in 2019-22, 26.44 s in 2025; trend +15.7 ms/yr) is the 26.5 s component becoming dominant from ~Feb 2025. It is seen at both stations, and the 28 s line does not shift, so it is not an instrument/timing artefact. Component frequencies look stable to ~0.03 s over 2019-26 (limited by the stacked-spectrum grid of ~0.026 s). Overall median 26.341 s [26.336-26.347] (day-bootstrap 95% CI).
- Swell: daily EDA detection rate vs ERA5 Hs at 3N 8E: r=0.20 (lag 0), falling with lag. Detection is highest Apr-Aug (austral winter swell). Period does not track Hs (r=-0.01).
- Caveats: the tide comes from 2008-11 gauge constants (stationary-tide assumption) at one coastal point. The "fixed band" mixes both doublet components. Surrogate p-values are floored at 0.01 (100 surrogates) in source_vs_site. TAM's background modulation complicates using TAM as a pure noise reference. Overlapping 3-h windows mean n_eff ~ n/3 for error bars.
- Novelty check: a quick search found no published tidal-modulation result. The 2026 Nat Comms swell paper (s41467-026-71541-6) mentions tides only as an analogy for triggering.
- Next steps: (1) tide currents vs height: use a gridded tide model (TPXO/FES needs registration) at the Bioko/source location to pin down the phase lag; (2) separate the doublet components and test M2 on each; also test the 16 s and 28 s lines formally; (3) relate the modulation to tidal water depth over the shallow shelf with a simple primary-microseism-depth model vs a crack model (crack: mostly amplitude, little frequency change; check whether a 0.5 dB amplitude change without a frequency change is diagnostic); (4) add 2-3 more distant stations (e.g. GEOSCOPE/IU in other azimuths) for azimuthal consistency; (5) investigate the 2025 doublet-dominance switch (swell climate? source change?) — possibly a second short paper; (6) pre-register the M2 amplitude/phase and confirm on 2027 data.

- Physical toy model (pulse26/model.py, model.txt, fig9_tide_coupling_model.png). Linear Airy waves with shoaling, M2 a=0.59 m:
  * Bottom pressure and bed orbital velocity: WRONG SIGN (maximum at LOW water), |amplitude| <=0.1 dB (pressure) / <=0.2 dB (velocity) for h0>=20 m.
  * Primary-microseism wavy-bottom coupling (power ~ p_b^2 * k^-n, n=2-3): right sign (max at HW), but only +0.01-0.11 dB at 20-50 m for 26 s. Reaching +0.45 dB needs h0 ~3-5 m (n=2) or ~5-8 m (n=3).
  * Depth-limited (breaking) forcing ~h^2: +0.5 dB at ~10 m, right sign. But the cm-high 26 s swell does not break; this applies only if energetic shorter swell in the surf zone matters.
  * Direction-dependent current (standing tide, U=a*w*L/h0): <=0.02-0.07 dB at 20-50 m, up to 0.19 dB at 10 m with a 50 km shelf. Peaks at +3.1 h (opposing ebb). The +1.7-2.0 h lag would need current/height ratio 1.2-1.6.
  * Threshold/exponential triggering, rate ~ exp(dp/p0): observed size implies p0 ~ 50-65 kPa (tidal bottom-pressure amplitude 5.9 kPa).
  => Linear hydrodynamic depth effects at shelf depths (>=20 m) are about 4-40x too small [corrected 11 Oct: 0.47/0.112 to 0.47/0.011] (or the wrong sign). The observed +0.45 dB at HW needs either coupling in very shallow water (<=5-10 m: nearshore, surf zone, estuaries; tidal HW there can lag the open coast by hours, which could also explain the +1.7-2 h lag), or a nonlinear/threshold source response with p0 ~ 50-65 kPa. Lag is unexplained by height alone. Small M4 argues against |current| control.
