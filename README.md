# AI research experiments

AI-generated research manuscripts and supporting artifacts from experiments initiated and guided by [Larsen Cundric](https://github.com/LarsenCundric).

The AI systems produced the mathematical arguments, code, and manuscript text. Larsen's role is organizing and guiding the experiments and sharing the results. He does not claim to have developed the proofs or independently verified the mathematics.

These materials are shared for scrutiny, correction, and further work. Inclusion here is not a claim that a result is correct, novel, or peer reviewed.

Each experiment has its own directory with its manuscript, provenance, available supporting files, and verification status. More experiments can be added without changing the existing records.

## Review or take a paper further

Mathematicians and other researchers are welcome to examine the arguments, report gaps, reproduce computations, or discuss preparing a verified version for publication. [Open an issue](https://github.com/LarsenCundric/ai-research-experiments/issues) and identify the paper and the relevant theorem, file, or computation.

Larsen is not seeking mathematical coauthorship for initiating these experiments. An acknowledgment of that role would be sufficient. Any future authorship should reflect the actual contributions to the eventual work, with transparent disclosure of the AI's contribution.

## Reading the evidence

- AI-generated internal reviews are not independent human peer review.
- Finite computational checks do not establish a general theorem.
- Any formal verification applies only to the statements actually checked.
- Historical artifacts can contain superseded claims, terminology, and attribution. Each paper's README identifies the current manuscript and known limitations.

No repository-wide reuse license has been selected yet. Please open an issue to discuss adapting or republishing the materials.
